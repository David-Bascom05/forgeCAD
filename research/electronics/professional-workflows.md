# What would make a professional Altium team try ForgeCAD?

Evidence review, 25 November 2026. The strongest evidence comes from two engineers describing actual migrations, a design office publishing its operational workflow, and a design house specifying what its clients receive. Several other sources document feature requests and current competitor behavior. The order below reflects how decisively each requirement could stop a particular adoption. Public evidence does not establish what fraction of Altium users would switch.

## Features users have specifically asked for

| Request and strength of signal | Evidence and existing behavior | Concrete opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| **Carry functional context into placement. Strong recurrence.** | An April 2019 placement thread, a separate July 2020 discussion, a community-built plugin and a later feature request describe the initial manual task of regrouping related footprints. Users disagree about copying absolute schematic positions. The newer issue specifically calls out repeated stages, decouplers, unchanged existing placements and global GND joining unrelated groups. [2019 thread](https://forum.kicad.info/t/placement-of-parts-when-starting-routing/16274) · [2020 discussion](https://forum.kicad.info/t/auto-place-components-where-they-appear-in-the-schematic/23936) · [Plugin author's description](https://forum.kicad.info/t/schematic-positions-to-pcb-positions-plugin/23528) · [Specific enhancement request](https://gitlab.com/kicad/code/kicad/-/work_items/25000) | Starting from a hand-drawn schematic, cross-probe a functional block, inspect the particular pin a capacitor serves, and preserve confirmed intent through board movement. Ask only when ambiguity affects the decision. |
| **Keep placement, routing and checks synchronized. Strong recurrence for individual operations.** | Multiple users describe difficulty moving routed components and retaining attached tracks. Another asks for the router to walk around selected filled zones to avoid dividing them. Respondents point out that normal pours intentionally adapt to obstacles. KiCad 10 already highlights colliding courtyards on Move and constrains interactive route dragging. Altium offers Online DRC and documents which actions omit polygon checks when the pour is stale. Show the tracks retained, exact obstacle and limiting rule. Expose whether the nearby copper island is up to date and whether it still connects. Make the appropriate filled-copper policy selectable. |
| **Set rules graphically and understand what they measure. Direct current evidence.** | In an April 2026 graphical DRC editor discussion, an Altium user specifically values its visual geometric rule explanations and query helper. KiCad 10 includes a graphical rule editor. [Designer feedback](https://forum.kicad.info/t/graphical-drc-editor-suggestions-wanted/68409) · [KiCad 10 PCB editor](https://docs.kicad.org/10.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html) | Select two pins and choose the relationship in the GUI. At violation time show the selected endpoints, measured value and the rule source. Make these edits round-trip through typed source. |
| **Review changes in a schematic, layout and system assembly. Strong tool-building signal.** | KiRI provides a visual comparison of schematic sheets and PCB layers between Git revisions. Its repository showed 724 stars and 49 forks when consulted. Kinetic Vision's named account above independently identifies reviewers outside the electrical team. Stars indicate interest; they do not measure paid demand. [KiRI](https://github.com/leoheck/kiri) | Show what a manufacturing or mechanical reviewer needs to approve. A connector moved 1.2 mm, its pinout stayed the same, its enclosure clearance fell, and its pick-and-place XY changed. Keep a navigable witness for each assertion. |
| **Check mechanical context against the editable board. Strong independent signals.** | In November 2026, a designer again requested a way to load an enclosure STEP file and check it against the board. | **Keep a human-drawn sheet legible. Direct, narrower evidence.** | A designer reports manually repositioning reference text when it overlaps other symbols or wires or becomes ambiguous. Existing KiCad fixes address particular text-placement cases. The broad opportunity for a semantic readability checker is a hypothesis worth testing with professional reviewers. [User's example](https://forum.kicad.info/t/eeschema-default-text-positioning-is-there-a-way-to-get-the-old-behaviour/56268) · [KiCad regression fixtures](https://gitlab.com/kicad/code/kicad/-/tree/10.0.0-rc1/qa/data/eeschema?ref_type=tags) | Start with demonstrable cases: unreadable labels, apparently connected pins and ambiguous junctions. ## How much does speed matter?

The Printed Circuit Engineering Association's 2024 publication reports that among more than 100 participating designers, **56% identified workload as a top challenge** and **57% used Altium at least weekly**. These percentages describe respondents to a trade publication's survey. An April–May 2026 web survey of **400 North American engineers**, run by EETech Research and commissioned by component manufacturer Weidmüller, reports real-time error detection among open-ended wishes for AI tools. Its public summary gives no count for that answer. These sources support testing time savings and immediate feedback. They do not quantify willingness to switch for 60 Hz DRC. [PCEA survey](https://digital.pcea.net/issues/august-2024/salary-survey/) · [Survey methods and results](https://www-prod.isa.org/web/acom/article/weidmuller-usa-national-pcb-design-survey-pcb-design-tools-ai-tool)

Ask a designer to make ten typical changes on an existing product, using their own libraries, constraints and outputs. Count edits that require a manual check, missed violations, incorrect warnings, time to the first trustworthy explanation, and time to a release a colleague approves. A 5 ms answer to the wrong electrical or mechanical question has no value in this test. Include at least one edit that requires genuine global work and verify that the review communicates the outstanding coverage honestly.

## Three entry points for a professional pilot

1. **Existing released Altium board.** Start with read-only analysis. Compare intended connections, effective rules, pad maps, copper, drills and manufacturing coordinates against the release evidence, then perform the enclosure and harness check. Flag unsupported inputs and show the impact of each discrepancy. If the client requires native Altium authoring, keep that editor in the loop.
2. **New board at a team with a large approved library.** Integrate a selected library subset and release job, retain upstream IDs and prove a clean repeatable manufacture handoff. Run side-by-side with the existing PDM or PLM. Altium currently documents a KiCad import into its review workspace and bidirectional PLM exchanges covering parts, choices, metadata and released files. Interview the librarian and release owner as well as the designer. [Current Altium platform contracts](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-365/quicknav-key-capabilities)
3. **Repeated power or sensor channel with a mechanical assembly.** Draw the schematic using standard parts. Place one channel, inspect the inferred pin-specific relationships, drag a bypass capacitor and a connector, then regenerate and compare the release. Test connectivity, distances, witness quality and end-to-end frame time together. A designer may prefer ForgeCAD for this work even while a different product line keeps its incumbent tools.

For each prospect, ask for the last actual respin, library migration or release that went wrong, the paid hours involved, who could veto a switch, and one archived design with its accepted outputs. Capture requirements for SI/PI, rigid-flex, access control, deployment and support in that customer's words. The public evidence supports several of these as decisive for individual teams. It does not support presenting a universal list of enterprise requirements.

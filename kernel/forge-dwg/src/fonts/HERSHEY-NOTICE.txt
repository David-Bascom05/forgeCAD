hershey.bin: the Hershey fonts' glyph data

hershey.bin holds the glyphs of twenty faces of the Hershey fonts (rowmans, rowmand,
rowmant, timesr, timesi, timesib, scripts, scriptc, gothiceng, gothicger, gothicita,
greeks, greekc, cyrillic, mathlow, mathupp, astrology, meteorology, music and symbolic),
packed by `cargo run -p forge-dwg --example dwgfonts -- pack JHF_DIR hershey.bin`.

The data comes from the Usenet distribution of the Hershey fonts (comp.sources.unix,
1986), in the .jhf files that Jeff Epler published at
http://emergent.unpythonic.net/software/hershey, as kept in
https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts (directory hershey-fonts/, whose COPYING
puts the .jhf files under the terms below; the GPL in that repository covers only its
library code, which is not used here). Only the glyph data was taken: no code.

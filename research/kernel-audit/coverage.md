# Coverage of the flat-face boolean

Kernel: `kernel/forge-bool` at 888fc195, unchanged on the branch since then. Sources:
- Lean: `research/kernel-audit/coverage/`;
- the Aeneas extraction: `coverage/aeneas/`;
- the Rust harness: `verify/forge-coverage/`;
- the exact spec: `verify/forge-exact/` (ForgeExact, the spec agent's).

**Question.** Does `forge_bool::boolean` cover every case of the flat path, meaning every
consistent sign pattern of the predicates it evaluates? Where it doesn't, which kernel site fails,
on what geometry, and with what repro?

**Short answer.**
- On the box-box family, every consistent pattern is covered except one:
  - two boxes meeting at a single corner and united (8 sign patterns, one configuration up to
    mirroring);
  - the kernel returns a pinched body there, where the exact answer is ZeroThickness.
- The modelled kernel's answer on all other patterns is the spec's regularised boolean.
- Inside the tolerance band (inputs whose coincidences are within LINEAR), five bands fail. Their
  sites are pinned:
  - one is a disagreement between `on_edge`, `share_cuts`/`place_cuts` and `point_in_face`;
  - two are one probe floor in `clear_step`;
  - one is the coplanar imprint's stretch samples (G5a), one `nearly_flush`'s NEAR cap (G5b).
- Tilted planes:
  - across the 1e-11, 1e-9, 1e-6, 1e-4 and 1e-3 thresholds, faces that share no boundary are right
    at every angle;
  - a tilted face whose sides are flush with the other body's fails in a band set by
    `nearly_flush`'s LINEAR and the same probe floor;
  - a face drafted or tilted against a face it nearly lies on fails at draft-like angles
    (0.006°–0.103° for a drafted lid) in two bands, G5a and G5b, each pinned to its site and
    reproduced by a real `draft(...)` part (`parts/drafted_lid.fg`).

## 1. What is proven

### 1.1 The box-box family (Coverage/Model.lean, Coverage/Theorems.lean)

`Model.lean` is a hand model of the flat path on two axis-aligned boxes. Each step cites the
boolean.rs and intersect.rs lines it models:
- face pairs;
- `trim`'s edge loop and stretch test;
- coplanar imprint;
- `coincide` / `share_cuts`;
- regions;
- `classify_specs`: coplanar, lean, flood, ray;
- `keep_of`;
- the four-use ZeroThickness test (1739–1749; the first session cited 1724–1737, and the touch test as
  1061–1094 where it is 1067–1108. boolean.rs is unchanged since 888fc195).

Every predicate on two boxes compares two coordinates on one axis. So a consistent sign assignment
is one of Allen's 13 relations per axis, and with three ops that gives 13³ × 3 = 6591 patterns.

| theorem | statement | checked by |
|---|---|---|
| `Rel.complete`, `Rel.count` | the 13 relations are exactly the consistent rank tuples of one axis | kernel (`decide +kernel`) |
| `flags_inert` | CAREFUL, CHORDS, NO_RUN_ON, FENCES and ABSORB_HAIR_ROOTS change nothing a flat pair reaches | kernel |
| `no_retry` | no consistent pattern, on either sample side, ends Lost or Unsupported, so `plan` (889), `plan_retried` (910) and `plan_fenced` (949) never enter their ladders | `native_decide` |
| `sample_inert` | the stretch sample's free side never matters | `native_decide` |
| `gaps` | the patterns whose modelled outcome is not the exact one are exactly the 8 corner-touch unions, each `pinched` where exact is `zeroThickness` | `native_decide` |
| `correct_off_gaps` | every other pattern: modelled outcome = exact outcome | `native_decide` |

The full `lake build Coverage.Theorems` takes 724 s.

`native_decide` trusts the Lean compiler and its runtime, not only the kernel: these four are
proofs by compiled evaluation.

**Model vs kernel.** The real kernel (`box_table`) gives the model's outcome on all 6591 patterns,
the gap set included:
- at unit 10 this session;
- at 0.001, 1, 7.3, 10 and 1000 in the first run (`results/kernel-unit10.tsv`, `results/model.tsv`).

### 1.2 The exact spec (Coverage/Correct.lean)

`ForgeExact.regBool o A B` is reg(A op B), where reg = closure ∘ interior in the topology of ℚ³
generated by open half-spaces. `reg_kept` states that any finite compatible decomposition's kept
cells regularise to it.

**`exact_is_regBool`.** The model's `exact` reads its answer off `kept3` on rank cells.
- Realise any valid pattern (not only the 6591) as two open boxes over ℚ with ends at their ranks.
- The 5³ open grid cells of the planes x, y, z ∈ {0..3} form a `Decomposition` compatible with both
  boxes. The walls are nowhere dense by `Flat.wall_nowhereDense`.
- The regularised union of the cells the model keeps is `regBool` of the two boxes.
- `keep_kept3` shows those are `Model.kept3`'s cells.
- Kernel-checked.

**`kernel_correct`.** For every pattern in `Pat.all` off the corner-touch unions, the conjunction of:
- `kernel p = exact p` (from `correct_off_gaps`, `native_decide`);
- the cells `exact` is read from regularise to `regBool`.

By construction, `Model.kernel` returns `.ok` only when its kept boundary equals `exactBoundary p`,
the faces between kept and dropped grid cells.

### 1.3 Tilted planes (Coverage/Tilt.lean, kernel-checked)

The flat path's angle thresholds are modelled as decisions of θ. θ is a natural number in units of
1e-13 rad; lengths are in mm.

| decision | site | test |
|---|---|---|
| `parallel` | intersect.rs:846, 853 | sin θ ≤ ANGULAR = 1e-11 |
| `leanSilent` | boolean.rs:5926 | lean ≤ 1e-9 |
| `runOn` | boolean.rs:2450 | sin θ ≤ 1e-6, and both edge ends within a vertex tolerance |
| `flush` | ssi.rs:66, 85 | sin θ ≤ 1e-4, and reach·θ ≤ LINEAR |
| `smooth` | boolean.rs:4077 | sin θ ≤ 1e-3 on a straight edge |
| `probeable` | boolean.rs:6186–6188 | a strip whose probe width is at least the last `clear_step` step, 0.05·2⁻¹¹ mm |

Theorems:
- `complete`: the decision vector is constant between breakpoints, for every θ.
- `gap_iff` / `gap_band`: a gap ⟺ flush sides ∧ reach·θ > LINEAR ∧ reach·θ < 0.05·2⁻¹⁰ mm.
- `disagree_iff`: the pair path's `closed_or_flush` and `ssi::coincident` (read across edges and by
  run-on) disagree on θ ∈ (ANGULAR, min(NEAR, LINEAR/reach)].
- `disagree_harmless`: the model predicts no gap in that band.
- `thresholds_reached`: each threshold is crossed in the swept range.

`tilt_compare.sh` checks the model against the kernel on:
- 166 angles from 1e-13 to 0.5, dense at every edge;
- 7 families × 3 ops (`verify/forge-coverage/src/tilt.rs`, exact by convex clipping).

The model's gap set equals the kernel's except at θ = 2e-6 exactly. There reach·θ = LINEAR in exact
arithmetic, and the kernel's floats land a hair past it.

### 1.4 `place_cuts` through Aeneas (coverage/aeneas)

**Translation.** `place_cuts`' clustering (boolean.rs:4561–4620) is extracted float-free
(`placecuts/src/lib.rs`), with its distance tests passed in as tables `le1/le2[i][j][a]` (d(i,j) ≤
tol_a, ≤ 2·tol_a):
- d ≤ max(x, y) ⟺ d ≤ x ∨ d ≤ y, and doubling is exact. This is argued in lib.rs, not proven.
- The merge measures a cut against both heads with the heads' reach, not the cut's own. The first
  extraction had this wrong.
- Pipeline: charon 9e193f0 (the aeneas pin, with the minirust submodule) → LLBC → aeneas 2e3d4488
  → `Placecuts/Funs.lean`, unedited.

**Proofs** (no sorry, no native_decide, standard axioms only):
- `place_spec`: on sized tables, `place` returns ok (no out-of-bounds, overflow or divergence) a
  vector of length n equal to a pure reference. Proven loop by loop with `loop.spec_decr_nat`.
- `place_exact`, **decide once**: if `near` is an equivalence, then cluster[i] = cluster[j] ⟺
  near i j. This is the model's exact clustering, so the model's identification of points by cell is
  what the kernel computes whenever coincidence is transitive.
- `place_chain`:
  - cuts at 0, 2.4, 1.2, 0.6, 1.8 with reach 1;
  - `near` links all five, yet `place` returns [0, 1, 0, 0, 1];
  - that is two vertices 0.6 reach apart, where union-find gives one.

  Non-transitive coincidence is where the tolerance band lives (§3).

### 1.5 `interior_point`'s probe (Coverage/Probe.lean, kernel-checked)

`clear_step` (boolean.rs:6179–6207) and its fallback `probe` (6174) are modelled on a strip of width
w, over ℚ. The steps are s_k = 2^(n−1−k)·u for k < n, where u is the last step ≥ 2·LINEAR. A probe is
clear when the far side is at least a step away; with no clear step, the fallback default step is
used.

| theorem | statement |
|---|---|
| `fits_iff` | some step fits ⟺ 2·u ≤ w |
| `kernelProbe_inside_iff` | the kernel's probe lands strictly inside the strip ⟺ 2·u ≤ w ∨ s_def < w |
| `g3_edge` | at a half chord ≥ 5 (s₀ = 0.05, 12 steps, u = 0.05·2⁻¹¹), a strip w ≤ 0.05 wide gets its probe outside ⟺ w < 0.05·2⁻¹⁰ mm = 4.8828125·LINEAR (`g3_edge_linear`) |
| `ProbeSpec` | **the specification for the fix-probe session's exact interior point**: strictly inside every strip of positive width |
| `kernel_not_spec` | the kernel's probe violates `ProbeSpec`: a strip 2·LINEAR wide |
| `midpoint_spec` | the midpoint meets `ProbeSpec` |

G3 and G4 are this theorem with w = δ (G3) and w = (reach/2)·θ (G4).

**On the extracted code** (`aeneas/clearstep`, `aeneas/lean/Clearstep`; no sorry, no native_decide,
standard axioms only). `interior_point`/`clear_step` are extracted float-free. Every float test is a
table: `small`, `nsteps`, `probed`, `clear`, `fallback_ok`. The kernel's skip of a piece by index is
kept, so a spur's piece is skipped both ways. Aeneas translates the extraction, and on it:
- `interior_point_spec`: on sized tables, it returns ok, equal to a pure reference.
- `first_clear_step`, `chosen_gets_step`: the step taken is the first step clear of every other
  piece; the chosen half-edge gets one whenever some step is clear.
- `strip_step_iff`, `strip_first_fit`, `strip_no_fit`, `g3_edge_code`: on a strip with 12 steps
  (lengths in units of the last step), a step is taken iff w ≥ 2 units = 0.05·2⁻¹⁰ mm, and otherwise
  the fallback runs.

Not proven: that the tables encode the kernel's floats (argued in lib.rs), and the conversion from
real widths to units (exact, because each 2·step is a whole number of units).

## 3. The tolerance band in the decision log

`coverage/decisions/box_decisions.rs` runs the band through the `decisions` feature. The feature
is on branch claude/repo-review-mbdrlj-decisions, used read-only; that branch's line numbers are
mapped to HEAD above. Outputs are in `results/decisions-*.txt`.

**Band 1 (two axes, 0.85·LINEAR).** All 1323 failing runs have contradictions. The ones that
separate failing from passing runs, as failing / passing counts:

| contradiction | failing | passing |
|---|---|---|
| `point_in_face` nearest edge vs itself: one site, the rule not transitive | 1323 | 0 |
| `on_edge` in `share_cuts` vs `place_cuts` end vertex | 1176 | 0 |
| `on_edge` in the stretch test vs `point_in_face` | 657 | 0 |

Many other contradictions occur in passing runs too (294 passing runs have one) and predict nothing.

**Band 2 (one axis, 1.5 and 4·LINEAR).**
- At 4·LINEAR, 527 of 527 failing runs log no contradiction. The probe step is not a tolerance
  decision, so the log cannot see G3.
- At 1.5·LINEAR, 389 of 527 failing runs log none; the rest show `point_in_face` contradictions
  that passing runs share.

**The ~4.8·LINEAR outer edge** is `clear_step`'s floor: 0.05·2⁻¹⁰ mm = 4.8828125·LINEAR exactly.
- Measured edge: 4.8828 fails, 4.8829 passes, on every axis and for δ < 0.
- It scales with the region: at unit 1 the half chord is 0.5 and the edge is 0.005·2⁻⁶ =
  7.8125·LINEAR (measured: 7.80 fails, 7.82 passes).
- Doubling any of six other candidate constants in a scratch tree left the edge where it was:
  - trim's 1.2 shallow factor (2846);
  - `edge_box` 2·slack (2388);
  - the 4·LINEAR sample break (3006, 3023);
  - 10·LINEAR (2666);
  - RIM (279);
  - 2·LINEAR at 2689 and 4247.
- The duplicate vertex appears straight after `apply`, before either pinch split.

## 4. The scoreboard's `invalid` for the corner touch

`tools/reliability/baseline.txt` says `plane_plane/vertex_on_vertex/Add invalid invalid`. The
scoreboard itself does not:
- At HEAD it reads `ok ok`.
- At the repository's root commit 34712b8 it also reads `ok ok`. That is the commit
  `baseline.txt` was added in, and it has not been regenerated since.
- The line predates the history: the pinch splitting (`split_old_pinches`) that makes the body
  valid is already in the root commit.
- `examples/scoreboard_corner` runs the scoreboard's own part, raw and `canonical`, on both regen
  paths. Every run gives a valid 12-face pinched body, as the bare boolean does.
- Three point-touch rows are stale the same way:
  - `vertex_on_vertex/Add`;
  - `cone_cone/perpendicular_apex_to_apex/Add`;
  - `cylinder_cone/apex_on_top_rim/Add`.
- `plane_torus/standing_tangent_top_inside/Keep` has moved from invalid to error.

The gate only flags regressions from ok/reject, so stale `invalid` rows never fail it. Regenerating
the baseline would show G1 as `ok` (wrong: exact is ZeroThickness).

## 5. Retry rungs that only gap patterns reach

- **Consistent box patterns** never enter the ladder (`no_retry`). No rung fires anywhere on the
  covered family.
- **Band 1 (G2)**: every failing run enters ABSORB_HAIR_ROOTS → CHORDS → NO_RUN_ON, and every rung
  ends `Internal`. None rescues a band input.
- **Bands 2 and 3 (G3, G4)**: these end in invalid bodies or ZeroThickness, not `Internal`, so the
  ladder is not entered. The exception is the SplitCrossed lower edge of G4, which reaches the
  ladder and is not rescued.
- **G5a in `cap`** (InconsistentClassification) enters ABSORB_HAIR_ROOTS and CHORDS, both ending
  `Internal`. The drafted lid's G5a and both G5b refusals are not `Internal`, so they do not enter
  the ladder.
- **CAREFUL, FENCES, the RETRIED result check and `bends`** are reached by no flat-face input here.
  CAREFUL needs a torus or procedural face (896); FENCES needs springs.

So on flat faces, every rung that fires at all fires only for gap inputs, and none of them helps.
This matches the decision log's finding: FENCES and `bends` never fire; ABSORB_HAIR_ROOTS, CHORDS
and CAREFUL never succeed.

## 6. Float-free kernel functions to extract for Aeneas

Each takes its distance or sign tests as tables, as `placecuts` does:

1. `place_cuts`' clustering (4561–4620). **Done**, proven (§1.4).
2. `clear_step` / `interior_point`'s step ladder (6150–6207). **Done** (§1.5): the geometric
   model and the extraction, each proven.
3. `edge_split` (756–813): the sort, dedup and piece chain. Cut order and the vertex keys are
   given.
4. The four-use count (1739–1749) and `keep_of` (6096).
5. `classify_specs`' flood (5933–6031): the union over shared native pieces, the lean seeds, and
   the InconsistentClassification refusal.
6. `split_new_pinches` / `split_old_pinches` fan walks (631–699): the coedge partner graph as
   input. This is where G1's pinched body is made.
7. The retry ladder control (889–962), with the flags as parameters. `flags_inert` and `no_retry`
   would then be statements about the code, not the model.

## 7. Trust base

- `Model.lean` is a hand model. It is line-referenced, and agreement with the kernel is checked
  pattern by pattern (§1.1), not proven.
- The box-family theorems use `native_decide`; the spec's theorems and `exact_is_regBool` are
  kernel-checked.
- The tilt model abstracts each site to its threshold comparison. Its predictions are checked
  against the kernel on 3486 runs.
- Aeneas proofs: the Rust→LLBC→Lean translation is trusted, and so is the float-to-table encoding
  (§1.4).

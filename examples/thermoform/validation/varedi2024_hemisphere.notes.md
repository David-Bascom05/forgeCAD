# varedi2024_hemisphere: assumptions

- Mould: male hemisphere R125 (the part's inside: from "inner"). Clamp window 430 x 430: flange 85 + margin 5.
- Sheet t0 3.0 nominal (measured 2.91 +-0.05). Temperature at contact 140.2 C centre / 131.5 C edge (IR): modelled
  uniform 136 C, library ABS Ogden (150 C fit). Mould 80 C (no cooling model; sticking contact).
- Process: the mould RISES into the clamped sheet (frame 103 mm high) then vacuum 77.7 kPa: modelled as the clamp
  coming down over the mould to the table, then 0.0777 MPa. Pre-contact sag (heating) not modelled.
- Data y is the camera-frame y (horizontal distance from the pole axis, it compresses near y = 125 as a projected
  arc): compared by the node's horizontal distance from the axis along the y axis (cut 90 deg). Ultrasonic rows only.

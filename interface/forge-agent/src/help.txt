## act lines add param before edit set dim rm mv suppress unsuppress rename undo redo select
act F 'LINE' 'LINE'..  (or lines on stdin): all apply or none, one reply.
LINE                   any .fc line goes on the end; a sketch runs to its `end`
NAME = EXPR [mm|deg]   sets the parameter, or adds it on the end
before FEAT LINE       a line put in above FEAT
edit FEAT LINE         FEAT's line replaced; its id and label stay, so references hold
set FEAT K TEXT        FEAT's Kth word after the kind
dim SKETCH ROW VALUE   a dimension: `dim plate dist l1 50` (the row less its value, or its number)
rm FEAT                the line out; refused while another line names it
mv FEAT FEAT2|end      FEAT above FEAT2; ids and names stay
suppress FEAT          FEAT and all that names it out of the build, kept as comments `# ~ID LINE`
unsuppress ID          that suppressed line back, with its id and so its names
rename FEAT NAME       a display name in a comment `#: NAME`; references go on using the label
undo | redo | select REF..   each alone in its act (`measure F sel` reads the selection)
--base N: refused with a short diff if the part is no longer version N (`query F since N`).
Reply: `ok v7; +4 faces, volume -120.4 mm3, valid; picked fillet1:4`, or the line, the reason and
`try:` a replacement that builds; a selector that picks nothing refuses too.
FEAT: a label, kind+count (`hole2` the 2nd hole), `fN` (the Nth line), or `#ID` (`id N` in the file).

## file id next comment label
Units mm, angles degrees (`1.2r` is radians). `#` starts a comment. `name:` labels a line.
`id N` before a line (written by `act`) is the id its names come from, kept when lines move;
leave it off new lines. A first line `next N` keeps ids of deleted lines from coming back.
A part may hold several bodies; see `help bodies`.

## params param expr expression
w = 40                      NAME = EXPR [mm|deg], defined above its use; any number below
d = w*3 + 1 mm              may be an expression: w/2, (h + 2), sqrt(a), min(a,b)
EXPR: + - * / ^ ( ) sqrt min max abs pi sin cos tan asin acos atan atan2(y,x) (degrees)

## sketch rows constraints
plate: sketch xy            sketch FRAME | sketch on REF (a planar face); rows to `end`
  rect 0 0 38 27            l1..l4 (corners shared, kept square)
  circle 20 12 4            c1
  pt 6 6                    p1
  fix l1.a                  constraints and dimensions, valued by EXPR:
  dist l1 w                   fix E.. | co A B | hor|ver E.. | par|perp|tan|conc A B
  dia c1 8                    eq A B.. | mid P L | sym P Q L | dist A B V | dist L V
  hdist l1.a c1 w/2           hdist|vdist P Q V | rad|dia C V | ang L M V
end
extrude 6 plate             its one region; `plate E`: the region E bounds outside
hole 3 on plate p1 thru     FRAME on a sketch's plane (-on: flipped), at its point
`ids NAME N ..` keeps those entities' segment ids (so their faces' names) through later edits.
Rows: `pt P` `line P P` `poly P P.. [open]` `rect X Y W H` `circle P R` `arc C S E [cw]` `spline P P.. [open]`
(cubic through them) `bspline D P P.. [knots K..] [weights W..] [open]` `ellipse C MAJOR B` `earc C MAJOR B S E [cw]`
`project REF..` (+`cons`: construction). P: `X Y` or `p1`, `l2.a`|`.b`, `c1` (centre), `s1.a`|`.b`|`.0`.. (a spline's points), `o`.
Entities count by kind: p l c a s e. `tan` takes a spline's end and the line or arc ending there, or an ellipse and a line.
`project`: 3D geometry above this line laid on the plane and held there, read afresh on every rebuild: REF an
edge, a face (its boundary edges), a vertex, a work point or anything else `help ref` allows. Results count on their own,
j1 j2.. whatever each comes to, and take rows like others (`dist j1.a l1.b 5`, `line j1.b 10 10`). A line gives a line, or a
point seen end-on; a circle or arc in a parallel plane the same, in a square-on plane a line. A tilted circle, an ellipse
or a general curve is refused: `project` doesn't make their images yet. A row whose source an edit took away is an error
naming it. A face or `(selector)` may give more or fewer curves after an edit, so rows can't name those: to constrain to
them list the edges, `project @e.. @e..`.
A row the rows above imply is an error; `ref dist l1` (no value) is driven. `query F check` shows
driven values, each sketch's freedom and conflicting rows. A sketch `on` something follows edits above it.

## extrude revolve ball ops op
[op] is add (default) cut keep (intersect) new (body); `new` for the first solid.
extrude [op] EXT (FRAME LOOPS | SKETCH [E])    EXT: N [taper A | along X Y Z] | sym N [along X Y Z] | two N M [along X Y Z] | thru [back|both] [along X Y Z] | next [back] | to FEAT [@face] [off N]
  along X Y Z: the walls run that way (world; any length or sense), each end N along the sketch's normal and parallel to it
  taper A: the sides lean in by A going up (negative: out), the profile as drawn at the sketch; ends where it closes up
revolve [op] [TURN] (FRAME [axis x|y|OX OY DX DY] LOOPS | SKETCH [axis L] [E])    TURN: like EXT, in degrees; default 360
ball [op] R FRAME
`next`: up to the next face met. `to`: a plane or sketch feature, or a feature's face (plane, cylinder..).
Solids added together keep faces that lie in one plane split (a known gap): one profile, `near` or handles pick one.

## frame xy xz yz at on
FRAME: [xy|xz|yz|-xy|-xz|-yz | z A B C [x A B C] | [-]on FEAT [P] | on REF] [at X Y Z | at POINT]     default: xy at 0 0 0
`at` is its origin in world coordinates, its z the extrude or drill direction; a hole's mouth is at the origin.
xy: normal +z; xz: sketch x,y = world x,z, normal -y; yz: world y,z, normal +x;
-xy: sketch y = world -y, normal -z (drill down); -xz: normal +y; -yz: normal -x

## loops rect rrect circle poly loop spline thru ellipse
LOOPS (first is outside, rest holes; X Y a corner): rect X Y W H | rrect X Y W H CORNER_R | circle CX CY R |
ellipse CX CY A B ROT [cw] | poly X Y X Y .. | spline.. | thru.. | loop (line AX AY BX BY | arc CX CY R START SWEEP |
spline D X Y .. [knots K..] [weights W..] | thru X Y .. [from DX DY] [to DX DY] | ell CX CY A B ROT START SWEEP)..
spline: B-spline of degree D on control points, uniform and clamped unless knots are given. thru: the cubic through
the points, closed when the first is the last. A corner in a curve splits its wall into faces.
Wherever LOOPS go (sweep, coil, loft profiles too), `SKETCH [E]` may stand instead.

## fillet radius setback from
fillet R [only] [from VREF] REF.. [R ..] [setback VREF [REF] D]..    a new radius applies onward; only: just these edges, not the tangent chains they lie on
R: 3 | 2..4 (start to end) | 0:2,0.5:4,1:2 (fraction:radius); the start is `from VREF`, else it is written in
(`from (verts near X Y Z)`); a constant radius holds along its own edge, a tangent chain varies over its
variable and unpicked edges; `setback VREF [REF] D`: blends meeting at a corner stop D from it.

## facefillet fullround
facefillet R REF.. to REF..   between two face sets (their edges, or the band of four-sided strip faces joining them goes)
fullround SIDE MIDDLE SIDE    the ball touching both sides and the middle takes the middle away, its radius
following the sides; a ring's top rounds all the way round.

## chamfer
chamfer D REF.. | chamfer D D2 face REF REF.. | chamfer D ang A face REF REF..

## shell hollow draft
shell T [out] REF..    hollow to walls T thick (out: outside the skin), REF faces open
draft A FRAME REF..    faces planar and meeting the neutral plane; draft before rounding their edges

## hole
hole (D | SIZE [iso|ansi] [close|normal|loose|tap] [cbore|csink]) (FRAME | [-]on SK pts [P.. | centers]) [cb D2 DEPTH | cs D2 A] (N | thru | next | to FEAT [@face] [off N]) [pt A] [thread SPEC]
N: depth, flat bottom unless `pt 118`; thru: through the whole part. SIZE: M6 M8x1 (ISO), 1/4-20 10-32
(UTS) from the tables (iso: DIN 336 tap drills, DIN 974-1 seats; ansi: tap D-P, ASME B18.3); fit normal
clearance, or tap (tap drill, thread recorded); cbore|csink seat a head. Other sizes: `hole D .. thread M36x2`.
A hole's thread is recorded only; `thread hole FEAT N` models it (bore it to the minor).
`on SK pts`: one hole at each of a sketch's points (as pattern pts), the first where the frame is.

## pattern lin circ mirror
pattern (body | FEAT..) (lin DX DY DZ N STEP [DX DY DZ N STEP] | circ OX OY OZ AX AY AZ N [A] [step] | mirror FRAME
  | path REF.. N (STEP|fit) [fixed] [rev] | pts (X Y Z | REF | SKETCH [P.. | centers])..)
N counts the original; body: the whole part. lin: STEP mm apart along DIR; circ: N over A (default
360) or A apart (`step`); mirror: in the frame's xy plane (`mirror yz`: across x=0). path: edges end
to end (FEAT @e @e..) or an axis, turned along it unless fixed. pts: the source at the first point;
SKETCH: its points P, or all its `pt`s (+ arc and circle `centers`), in order (`hole .. -on sk p1`)

## sweep coil loft path
sweep [op] FRAME LOOPS path PATH [extend N M | to FEAT [@face] [off N]]     profile square to the path's start
coil [op] FRAME [axis ..] LOOPS (pitch P turns N | turns N height H | pitch P height H | pitch P to FEAT [@face] [off N]) [taper A] [left]
loft [op] [smooth [start W] [end W]] [align auto|none] (profile FRAME LOOPS [seam X Y] | point X Y Z [dome DX DY DZ [W]]).. [rail I PATH.. | centre PATH]     I: vertex (where segment I starts)
`align auto`: each section starts where it comes nearest the one before's start. `seam X Y`: where this profile starts.
`point` first or last: the loft closes on it, sharp, or with `dome` (smooth lofts) rounded, its walls arriving square to D
PATH: (line AX AY AZ BX BY BZ | arc FRAME R START SWEEP | spline D X Y Z .. [knots K..] [weights W..]).. | SKETCH [E]     tangent-continuous, each curve in one plane; an arc's FRAME is its circle's, centre at `at`

## thread
thread (SPEC FRAME ext|int | SPEC face FEAT REF [flip] | hole FEAT) (N | full | to FEAT [@face] [off N]) [left] [chamfer] [runout]
modeled 60 deg ISO/UTS. SPEC: M6 (coarse) | M6x0.75 | 1/4-20 | 0.19-24 (inch, TPI). FRAME: origin on
the axis at the start, z into the part; ext round a rod of the major, int in a bore of the minor
(major - 1.0825 P; a tap drill, major - P, is close). face: from the cylinder face's end furthest
along its axis (flip: other); hole: a hole's recorded thread from its mouth, copies too. runout: past the end

## text
text [add|cut] STRING font PATH SIZE DEPTH FRAME [wrap R]

the origin along x, letters up y, DEPTH along z (cut: into the part). wrap R: round a cylinder about z

## rib web
rib T [pos|neg] [web] [flip] [draft A] FRAME (line AX AY BX BY | arc CX CY R START SWEEP)..
a wall along an open path, ends run on to the part, filling to the path's left (flip: right), T thick
along z (sym; pos|neg one side); web: fills along -z (flip +z), T across the path; draft: thicker inward

## offset thicken delete replace move
offset D REF..                   faces moved D along their outward normals, neighbours re-met

## bodies combine split splitface movebody copybody in
`new` (after the first), `split` (both sides kept unless `+` or `-`; `+` is the cutter's normal side, a new
body) and `copybody` make bodies; a body is named by the line that made it. Any line may end `in FEAT[,FEAT..]`
or `in all`; otherwise it acts on the last body made, and fillet, chamfer, draft, shell and splitface on the
bodies holding their refs. Bodies may overlap. `query F stat` lists each body's volume.
combine (add|cut|keep) TARGET TOOL.. [keep]      bodies; keep: the tool bodies stay
split BY [+|-]     splitface REF.. by BY     movebody MOTION..     copybody MOTION..
BY: FEAT [@face] | plane FRAME | curve FRAME (line AX AY BX BY | arc CX CY R A0 SWEEP | circle CX CY R)
MOTION: by DX DY DZ | rot OX OY OZ AX AY AZ A | frame FRAME      (applied in order)

## ref refs feat handles
REF: @handle | (selector), found on the part as it stands above that line; FEAT, a sketch's plane, FEAT @h|(sel)
(an entity it made), or SKETCH P: a sketch's point (`p1`, `l2.b`, `c1`); `at REF` puts an origin on any point.
Handles (`@`, f/e/v for face/edge/vertex, 8+ hex digits of the name) survive edits below their feature; one
whose entity an upstream edit took away fails, never finds another. Selectors survive more.

## selectors selector query
`faces|edges|verts`, then filters in order; inline in parentheses, or `query F SEL [--at FEAT]`.
plane cyl cone sphere torus blend spline | line circle curve      kind (fillets: cyl, or torus round a circle)
convex concave smooth                                      how an edge's faces meet
normal +z | normal A B C | along +x                        plane normal; line direction
r=3 r<3 r>3   longer 5   shorter 5                         radius; edge length
of FEAT..   between FEAT FEAT   on @h   body FEAT          made by (copies too); joining; touching; in body
top bottom left right front back   near X Y Z              keep the extreme ones (ties too); nearest
query answers `@e78d2213f line -x len=60 at 0,20,40` (round: axis, centre; body=FEAT; faces on one surface
are one face; alike ones share a line). Also: query F stat | file | check | since N | dims.

## render labels
render F [VIEW] [--labels SEL] [--px 400] [-o F.png]   VIEW: iso (default) front top right back left bottom. px: long side
Rendered labels are handles without `@`.

## export step ap214 ap242
`forgecad export F [ap214|ap242] [tol MM] [-o F.step]`: the part as a STEP file, an advanced B-rep.
Planes, cylinders, cones, spheres, tori and conics are written exact. Fillets, lofts, threads and
other surfaces made by construction become bicubic B-splines within tol (default 0.0001 mm);
the reply says how many and how far they strayed.

## commands build check diff serve mcp
forgecad act|query|measure|render|help as above; also
forgecad build F     ok, or error at file line N + hint; exact volume, area; bbox; bodies (`query F stat`)
forgecad check F     each body's validity after each line (`query F check`)
forgecad diff A B    what B changed from A
forgecad serve|mcp [--root DIR] [--writable]   stdio JSON-RPC / MCP, .fc files under DIR (default .) only; act needs --writable
`serve` takes one JSON object per line, `{"id":1,"method":"build","params":{"file":"b.fc"}}`; params: file sel at what to view labels px other lines base.

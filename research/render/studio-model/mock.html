Render Studio › Model, redesigned (mock)

  • Appearance brush: one active swatch. Pick it from the palette (search, by family); click parts in the view to apply; Esc stops. Drag a swatch onto a row or the view does the same.
  • Parts: the model's tree (assembly → browser groups → parts; a single part → its bodies), collapsed, filterable, virtualised. Each row: its look as a chip (dashed ring = its material's own), name, count. Ctrl/Shift multi-select → Apply / Reset. Clicking a chip opens the palette for that row.
  • Overrides: only what was changed, with Reset each / all.
  • Lighting, Camera, Output fold to one-line summaries; each part's default comes from its own material (today every part takes the assembly's).
  • No dropdown per part. 200+ parts: rows virtualised, frame time flat.

- Free inflation as a deep female cylinder (r 31.75, 130 deep, never reached): the sheet on the tool's top round
  the opening is stuck there, so the opening's sharp rim is the circular clamp.
- Law: the case's own Lodge single mode (G1 0.23959 MPa, tau 28.5 s, Tableau 1). The test lasts < 1 s << tau, so
  the instantaneous response is neo-Hookean with G = G1 (DERIVED). The paper's own model diverges after 0.63 s.
- Pressure: the measured maximum 0.02257 MPa; the model's 10 load steps (p/10 .. p) are compared with the
  measured h(p) interpolated. P = p a / (G t0) = 1.90 at the end: past the neo-Hookean limit point 1.879, so the
  last step(s) blow through (expected; the experiment is flow-controlled and dynamic).
- Compared: pole height h (mm) at each step; no thickness measured.
